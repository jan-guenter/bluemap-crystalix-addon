# Notices

This independently authored add-on interprets operator-installed Crystalix
and Fusion resources. It bundles no third-party models, textures, classes, or
binaries.

The small BlueMap adapter and mesh-emission mechanics follow the public MIT
BlueMap interfaces and the patterns used by the published BlueMap glass
add-ons in this workspace.

The add-on compiles four MIT-licensed sources from BlueMap Add-on Adapter API
`0.1.0-alpha.2`, commit `e81f08bc4bfbf02d810ec8949a019130e2e61634`.
Its standalone JAR is not bundled or installed.
